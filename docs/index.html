The Seven Steps

Run the steps in order — each builds on the last, and the notebook keeps its state between them. Every step ends with a short set of experiments; that is where the learning actually happens.

  1. 1

    The Dumbest Language Model

    5 min

    Ten lines. One word of memory. Fails so instructively you'll never forget why attention exists.

    You build A bigram babbler trained on Alice, and a live demonstration of two different contexts producing identical predictions.

    Open Step 1 in Colab ↗
  2. 2

    Turning Words Into Numbers

    20 min

    Write a tokenizer, break it on the word pizza, then meet GPT-2's real one.

    You build Word-level tokenizer → special tokens → byte-pair encoding → sliding-window dataset → token and position embeddings.

    Open Step 2 in Colab ↗
  3. 3

    Attention

    30 min

    The centre of the whole thing, built from a dot product upward.

    You build Scores → softmax → weighted sum; then trainable Q/K/V, the √d scaling, the causal mask, dropout, and multi-head attention — the exact class every later model uses.

    Open Step 3 in Colab ↗
  4. 4

    Assembling the Machine

    25 min

    LayerNorm, GELU, residual highways, one transformer block — then twelve of them.

    You build The real GPT-2 small architecture, 163M parameters, in about fifty lines. It speaks gibberish, because it has read nothing yet.

    Open Step 4 in Colab ↗
  5. 5

    Training WonderGPT

    35 min

    Watch a mind assemble itself out of arithmetic, one epoch at a time.

    You build Cross-entropy and perplexity, the five-step training loop, temperature and top-k, checkpoints — then OpenAI's real GPT-2 weights poured into your code, and a look inside all twelve of its heads.

    Open Step 5 in Colab ↗
  6. 6

    Wonderland or Baker Street?

    15 min

    Teach the model to tell Lewis Carroll from Arthur Conan Doyle.

    You build Swap the output head, freeze 94% of the model, fine-tune the rest. From a coin flip to ~81% on sentences it has never seen.

    Open Step 6 in Colab ↗
  7. 7

    Meet Cheshire

    20 min

    The trick that turned GPT-3 into ChatGPT, at miniature scale.

    You build An instruction dataset you can grade exactly, the loss mask that makes it work, and an assistant that follows orders — plus a front-row seat to where hallucination comes from.

    Open Step 7 in Colab ↗

Before you start

  1. Open the notebook with the tag above (or any Step's button — they all open the same notebook, scrolled to that Step).
  2. Runtime → Change runtime type → T4 GPU, then Save.
  3. Run cells top to bottom. The first one installs tiktoken; everything else is already on Colab.

Two switches live in Step 0: FAST = True shortens training runs, and LOAD_PRETRAINED in Step 5 controls the 670 MB GPT-2 download that Steps 6 and 7 rely on.

What it costs, what it saves

  • Colab files are temporary — books and checkpoints vanish when the runtime disconnects. Everything is re-downloadable.
  • To keep a model you trained, mount Google Drive and save into /content/drive/MyDrive/.
  • Runs on CPU too, at roughly 6–10× the time. If you're stuck on CPU, drop num_epochs in Step 5 to 5.
